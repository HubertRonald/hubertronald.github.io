## Attribution boundary

### Original project expression

The following are treated as project-specific expression where not otherwise attributed:

- prose authored specifically for this guide;
- editorial organization and terminology independently created for this repository;
- diagram concepts and human-directed visual composition;
- editorial annotations and explanatory captions;
- final curated visual artifacts, to the extent copyright or other applicable rights exist.

This category describes the **expression, direction, curation, and presentation**, not ownership of general FDE concepts or exclusive authorship of underlying source concepts.

### Synthesized concepts

The following are treated as concepts synthesized from the primary source corpus, not as inventions of this repository:

- capability areas used to describe FDE work;
- delivery lifecycle and deployment-oriented workflow;
- learning sequence and readiness progression;
- field/customer orientation and discovery practices;
- production, operations, and deployment emphasis;
- data, cloud, AI, integration, and field-deployment role variants;
- recurring engineering tradeoffs and evidence-oriented capstone practices.

The guide reconciles, renames, groups, and explains these themes in its own editorial structure.
